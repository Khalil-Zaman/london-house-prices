Explore the monthly series

The official UK House Price Index for every nation, English region and local-authority district — average price, or the pace of change on a year earlier.

Reading note: the HPI is a hedonic, mix-adjusted index of actual registered sales — smoother and slower than asking-price surveys. Northern Ireland's index is quarterly, so its districts repeat values within a quarter. The last couple of months revise as late registrations arrive.
